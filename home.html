About

A little about me

I've built my career in mortgage lending, growing from running a branch to leading a team of 45 loan officers and support staff. {{ABOUT_NOW}}

My approach with clients starts with listening. A home loan is one of the biggest financial decisions most people ever make, and you deserve to understand every part of it: your options, the real costs, and what happens next. My goal is to make every borrower feel like the most important person in my pipeline, because to me, you are.

As a leader, I focus on growing teams, mentoring loan officers, and building a culture where people share what works and everyone wins together. I'm also proud to serve on United Wholesale Mortgage's Advisory Council, where brokers give direct feedback on the tools and products we use every day.

Questions

Common questions

How much does it cost to talk with you?

Nothing. A conversation about your goals and options is free, and there's no obligation.

How long does it take to get pre-approved?

Often a day or two once I have your documents. Having pay stubs, W-2s or tax returns, and bank statements ready speeds things up.

What's the advantage of working with a mortgage broker?

A broker can compare loan programs across many wholesale lenders instead of one bank's menu, which means more ways to find the right fit for your situation.

Which states can you help me in?

I'm licensed in 34 states, including California, Texas, Florida, and Arizona. The full list is above, and you can verify it on NMLS Consumer Access.

I'm self-employed. Can I still qualify?

Often, yes. Depending on your situation, a traditional loan using tax returns or a bank statement loan may work.

Do you work with loan officers who want to join your team?

Yes. I'm growing my team and welcome confidential conversations. Visit the Join My Team page to learn more.
